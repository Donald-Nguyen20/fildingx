Howden IDF — Operation & Maintenance Manual
7.4 VIBRATION MONITORING AND PROTECTION
Each fan bearing housing is fitted with a velocity transducer mounted radially in the horizontal plane. Signals are routed to the vibration monitoring rack in the electrical building and from there to the DCS.

7.4.2 Protection settings
Alarm is set at 4.5 mm/s RMS and trip at 7.1 mm/s RMS, in accordance with ISO 10816-3 zone boundaries for Group 1 machines on rigid foundations. Both channels are voted 1-out-of-2 for alarm and 2-out-of-2 for trip.

Where the measured value exceeds the alarm level, the fan may continue in service provided the rate of increase is monitored and does not exceed 0.5 mm/s per week.
